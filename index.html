📜 Cultivation Guide

🌌 1. Breakthrough Mechanics

Advancing to higher cultivation realms requires three core elements:

  • Core Pill: Mandatory item. Pill grade increases your Realm Gift Multiplier.
  • Key Items: Specific realm requirements. Key items yield 50% (half) stat and gift value.
  • Offerings: Up to 3 additional treasures sacrificed to boost stat points and gift multiplier.
📍 2. Qi Spots & Gift Caps

Location limits your maximum Realm Gift Multiplier. Current qi spot and their ceiling are as follows:

Realm Modifiers (vs Current Realm):

  • +2 Realms Above: +50% Bonus (1.50x)
  • +1 Realm Above: +25% Bonus (1.25x)
  • Same Realm: 100% Standard (1.00x)
  • -1 Realm Below: -35% Penalty (0.65x)
  • -2 Realms Below: -70% Penalty (0.30x)
  • -3+ Realms Below: 0.25x Floor Cap (25% Min Value)

Item Stat Cap: Item stats stack linearly up to 6 points, then suffer a 50% diminishing return up to a max of 12 Stats. A maximum of 19 stats points can be obtained.

💡 4. How to Use
  • 1. Select your Current Realm (e.g., Qi Refining is R1, Foundation Establishment is R2, Qi Condensation is R3, etc.).
  • 2. Pick your Qi Spot location and Pill Grade.
  • 3. Set key items grades (Low, Mid, High, Heaven, Emperor).
  • 4. Choose up to 3 Offerings and their grades.
  • 5. Read real-time stats, gift multiplier, and foundation tier!
