📜 Cultivation Guide

🌌 1. Breakthrough Mechanics

When your cultivation bar reaches 100% at the peak of a realm (level 10, 20, 30, etc.), you must perform a breakthrough to advance.

Required Materials:

  • Core Pill: Required. Bought from Alchemist NPCs (up to High-Grade) or refined/dropped by bosses (up to Emperor-Grade).
  • Key Items: Required (1-2 per realm) until Immortal. Gives 50% stat points & 100% gift multiplier.
  • Offerings: Optional (up to 3 treasures). Sacrificed to boost stat points and gift multiplier.
📍 2. Qi Spots & Gift Caps

  • Low: 1.0x | Mid: 1.25x | High: 1.6x
  • Heaven: 2.2x | Emperor: 3.0x

Breakthrough Range Restrictions:

  • Standard Breakthrough: Restricted to offerings within ±2 realms of your current realm for all breakthroughs.
  • Foundation Establishment (R1 → R2): Offerings receive a special 1.5x Stat Point Multiplier for stat point calculations.

Item Stat Cap & Diminishing Returns:

  • 0 to 6 Stats: Every stat point gained gives full +1 Stat Point.
  • Above 6 Stats: Stat points suffer a 50% penalty (every 2 stat points give only +1 Stat Point).
  • Hard Cap: You can gain a maximum of 12 Stat Points from items.
  • Maximum Ceiling: Dao reaches 19 Total Stat Points (2 Base + 12 Items + 5 Dao). Hongmeng reaches 24 Total (2 Base + 12 Items + 10 Hongmeng).
💡 4. How to Use & Efficiency
  • 1. Select your Current Realm.
  • 2. Pick your Qi Spot location and Pill Grade.
  • 3. Set key items grades.
  • 4. Choose up to 3 Offerings and their grades.
  • 5. Read real-time stats, gift multiplier, and foundation tier!

Efficiency Percentage: Measures how close your setup is to the absolute maximum Realm Gift potential for your current realm (Current Realm Gift ÷ Max Realm Gift Potential).

⚡ 5. Foundation Remake

If you are unsatisfied with your foundation's realm gift, you can remake each realm's foundation once using a specialized pill.

Remaking to/within Hongmeng:

  • Requires a Hongmeng Remaking Pill.
  • Used to remake a Hongmeng-tier foundation, OR to upgrade any lower foundation (e.g., Dao, Earth) to Hongmeng. The better gift is kept.
  • Since Hongmeng realms (R11+) no longer use Key Items, a Remake in these realms only forfeits the Core Pill's multiplier. Your gift will rely entirely on your 3 Offerings + the Hongmeng Tribulation.

To compute your exact output, switch to Foundation Remake Mode using the toggle button above.

⚠️ Warning: Every major realm's foundation can only be remade a single time.
